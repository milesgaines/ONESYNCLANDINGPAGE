1. Introduction

OneSync Media LLC ("OneSync," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our music distribution platform and services.
This policy applies to all users of our website, mobile applications, and services. By using OneSync services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us:
  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth
  • Profile Data: Artist name, bio, social media links, profile photos
  • Payment Information: Bank account details, PayPal information, tax identification numbers
  • Identity Verification: Government-issued ID, business registration documents
  • Communication Data: Support tickets, feedback, survey responses

2.2 Content Information

Information related to your music and creative content:
  • Audio Files: Music tracks, metadata, artwork, liner notes
  • Distribution Data: Release information, streaming statistics, sales data
  • Rights Information: Publishing details, collaboration credits, licensing data
  • Performance Analytics: Play counts, geographic data, audience demographics

2.3 Technical Information

Automatically collected technical data:
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns, feature usage
  • Location Data: General geographic location based on IP address
  • Cookies: Session cookies, preference cookies, analytics cookies

3. How We Use Information

3.1 Service Provision

We use your information to provide and improve our services:
  • Distribute your music to streaming platforms and digital stores
  • Process payments and calculate royalties
  • Provide customer support and technical assistance
  • Maintain and improve platform functionality
  • Generate analytics and performance reports

3.2 Communication

We may use your contact information to:
  • Send important service updates and notifications
  • Provide marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Share industry news and platform updates

3.3 Legal and Business Purposes

We may use information for legitimate business purposes:
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security
  • Enforce our terms of service and policies
  • Conduct business analytics and market research

4. Information Sharing

4.1 Distribution Partners

We share necessary information with our distribution partners:
  • Streaming Platforms: Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, etc.
  • Digital Stores: iTunes, Google Play, Beatport, etc.
  • Rights Organizations: ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, and international PROs
  • Payment Processors: Banks, PayPal, and other financial institutions

4.2 Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers:
  • Cloud storage and hosting services
  • Analytics and marketing platforms
  • Customer support and communication tools
  • Security and fraud prevention services

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law:
  • Response to legal process, subpoenas, or court orders
  • Cooperation with law enforcement investigations
  • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
  • Prevention of fraud or illegal activities

5. Data Security

5.1 Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your data:
  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Access Controls: Multi-factor authentication and role-based access
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Data Backup: Secure, encrypted backup systems
  • Employee Training: Regular security awareness training

5.2 Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:
  • Account data: For the duration of your account plus 7 years for tax purposes
  • Music content: As long as distribution agreements are active
  • Payment records: 7 years for financial and tax compliance
  • Support communications: 3 years for service improvement

5.3 International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence. We ensure adequate protection through:
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with international partners
  • Adequacy decisions by relevant data protection authorities
  • Certification under recognized privacy frameworks

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

6.1 Types of Cookies

We use various types of cookies and similar technologies:
  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for website functionality and security
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how you use our website
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements

6.2 Cookie Management

You can control cookies through:
  • Browser settings to block or delete cookies
  • Our cookie consent banner preferences
  • Third-party opt-out tools for advertising cookies
  • Do Not Track browser settings (where supported)

6.3 Analytics

We use analytics services to improve our platform:
  • Google Analytics for website usage statistics
  • Custom analytics for platform feature usage
  • Performance monitoring for technical optimization

7. Your Privacy Rights

7.1 Access and Control

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:
  • Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (where applicable)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

7.2 Marketing Communications

Regarding marketing communications, you can:
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link
  • Update your communication preferences in your account settings
  • Contact us directly to opt-out of specific communications
  • Note: You cannot opt-out of essential service communications

7.3 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:
  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

7.4 European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have additional protections:
  • Right to lodge a complaint with supervisory authorities
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Enhanced rights for automated decision-making
  • Right to appoint a representative for privacy matters
