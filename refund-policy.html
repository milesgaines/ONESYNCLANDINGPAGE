2. Refund Eligibility

✓ Eligible for Refund
  • Service Failures: If we fail to deliver distribution services as promised
  • Technical Issues: Platform malfunctions preventing service access for 7+ consecutive days
  • Duplicate Charges: Accidental double billing for the same service
  • Unauthorized Charges: Charges made without your authorization
  • Service Cancellation: Within 7 days of initial payment for unused services
✗ Not Eligible for Refund
  • Change of Mind: General dissatisfaction after service delivery
  • Platform Rejections: Third-party platform rejections due to content issues
  • Artist Content Issues: Copyright claims or content violations
  • Completed Distributions: Services already delivered to streaming platforms
  • Promotional Services: Marketing campaigns already launched
  • Custom Services: Bespoke work completed to specifications

4. Service-Specific Refund Policies

4.1 Music Distribution

Distribution service refunds are subject to the following conditions:
  • Pre-Distribution: Full refund available if music hasn't been sent to platforms
  • Post-Distribution: Partial refunds may apply for future distribution periods
  • Annual Plans: Pro-rated refunds for unused months (minus setup fees)
  • Release Delays: Refunds available if delays exceed 14 days without cause

4.2 Promotional Services

Marketing and promotional service refunds:
  • Campaign Launch: No refunds once campaigns are live
  • Pre-Launch: Full refund available up to 24 hours before campaign start
  • Performance Guarantees: Partial refunds if guaranteed metrics aren't met
  • Third-Party Issues: Refunds available if platforms reject promotional content

4.3 Additional Services

Specialized service refund conditions:
  • Mastering Services: Full refund if initial master is rejected for technical issues
  • Artwork Creation: Revisions included; refunds only for non-delivery
  • Playlist Pitching: Refunds available if pitching service isn't performed
  • Radio Campaigns: Pro-rated refunds for unbroadcast segments

5. Refund Methods

5.1 Original Payment Method

Refunds are typically processed using your original payment method:
  • Credit Cards: 3-5 business days processing time
  • PayPal: 1-3 business days processing time
  • Bank Transfers: 5-10 business days processing time
  • Digital Wallets: 1-5 business days depending on provider

5.2 Alternative Methods

In cases where original payment method is unavailable:
  • Account credit for future services
  • Bank transfer to verified account
  • Check payment (for amounts over $100)
  • PayPal transfer to verified email

5.3 Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may apply in the following situations:
  • Services partially completed before cancellation
  • Pro-rated refunds for subscription services
  • Deductions for completed work or platform fees
  • Administrative fee deductions (up to $25)

6. Chargeback & Dispute Policy

Before initiating a chargeback, please contact us directly. We're committed to resolving issues promptly and fairly.

6.1 Chargeback Consequences

Unauthorized chargebacks may result in:
  • Immediate suspension of distribution services
  • Removal of music from streaming platforms
  • Account termination and blacklisting
  • Legal action for services already rendered

6.2 Dispute Resolution

We prefer to resolve disputes through direct communication:
  • Email support@onesync.music with detailed concerns
  • Request mediation through our customer service manager
  • Escalate to billing disputes team if necessary
  • Consider third-party mediation for complex cases

7. International Refund Considerations

7.1 Currency Exchange

For international transactions:
  • Refunds processed in original transaction currency
  • Exchange rate fluctuations may affect final amount
  • Additional bank fees may apply for international transfers
  • Processing times may be extended for international refunds

7.2 Regional Regulations

We comply with regional consumer protection laws:
  • EU Customers: 14-day cooling-off period for digital services
  • UK Customers: Consumer Rights Act protections apply
  • Australian Customers: Australian Consumer Law guarantees
  • Canadian Customers: Provincial consumer protection regulations
