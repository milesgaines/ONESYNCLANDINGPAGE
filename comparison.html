Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about choosing the right music distributor.

What is the best free music distribution service? +

OneSync is the best free music distribution service in 2026. Unlike other "free" services that take a percentage of your royalties or have hidden fees, OneSync offers truly free distribution with no annual fees, no per-release fees, and you keep 100% of your royalties. Plus, you get access to premium features like AI mastering, publishing administration, and sync licensing opportunities at no extra cost.

Is DistroKid worth it in 2026? +

DistroKid can be worth it if you only need basic distribution and release music frequently. However, at $22.99/year, the costs add up over time. More importantly, if you stop paying, your music gets removed from stores. Additionally, many features that are free with OneSync (like YouTube Content ID, mastering, and cover song licensing) cost extra with DistroKid. For most artists, a free service like OneSync offers better value.

What's the difference between TuneCore and DistroKid? +

TuneCore charges per release ($9.99/single, $29.99/album) while DistroKid charges a flat yearly fee ($22.99) for unlimited releases. TuneCore has been around longer and offers more stores, but their per-release model makes it expensive for artists who release frequently. DistroKid is more affordable for prolific artists but has fewer features. However, both pale in comparison to OneSync, which offers free distribution with more features than either platform.

Can I get an advance on my music royalties? +

Yes! OneSync offers royalty advances up to $750,000 for qualifying artists. This is significantly more than competitors like TuneCore's advance program. To qualify, you typically need a track record of consistent streaming numbers and releases. Advances allow you to fund recording, touring, marketing, and other career investments while OneSync recoups from your future royalties.

How long does it take to get music on Spotify? +

With OneSync and DistroKid, your music can be live on Spotify within 24-48 hours. TuneCore and CD Baby typically take 2-4 weeks. However, we recommend submitting your release at least 2-3 weeks before your release date to ensure time for playlist pitching and editorial consideration. OneSync's playlist pitching team can help maximize your chances of getting featured.

What is publishing administration and do I need it? +

Publishing administration helps you collect all the royalties you're owed from your songwriting, including mechanical royalties, performance royalties, and sync fees. If you write your own music, you're leaving money on the table without it. Most distributors charge extra for this service ($50-100/year), but OneSync includes it free. This can be worth thousands of dollars in uncollected royalties.

What is sync licensing? +

Sync licensing is when your music is used in TV shows, films, commercials, video games, and other visual media. A single sync placement can pay anywhere from $1,000 to $500,000+. OneSync offers sync licensing opportunities through our network of music supervisors—a feature most distributors don't offer or charge significant fees for. This can be a game-changing revenue stream for artists.

What happens to my music if I switch distributors? +

When switching distributors, you'll need to request a takedown from your old distributor and re-upload through your new one. This can cause a brief gap in availability. The good news is your streaming counts, saves, and playlist placements on Spotify are typically preserved if you use the same ISRC codes. OneSync makes switching easy with our migration support team who can help ensure a smooth transition.
