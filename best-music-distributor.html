Best Music Distributor 2026: Top 7 Services Ranked

We tested, compared, and ranked the top music distribution services for independent artists. Here's our definitive ranking for 2026.

Our #1 Pick: OneSync — free distribution to 400+ platforms, 100% royalties, sync licensing, publishing admin, and royalty advances up to $750K. No other distributor matches this combination.

How We Ranked These Distributors

We evaluated each distributor on five key criteria:

  1. Cost vs. Value: What you pay versus what you get
  2. Royalty Terms: How much of your earnings you keep
  3. Store Coverage: Number and quality of platforms reached
  4. Feature Set: Publishing admin, sync licensing, analytics, mastering tools
  5. Artist Experience: Speed, support quality, catalog control
#1

OneSync — Best Overall Music Distributor

Price: Free | Royalties: 100% | Stores: 400+

OneSync leads every category that matters. It's the only distributor that combines truly free distribution, 100% royalties, sync licensing, publishing administration, and royalty advances up to $750,000 — all without fees or revenue share. Releases go live in as fast as 1 hour, and the platform includes AI mastering and real-time analytics.

Best for: Every independent artist — from your first release to your hundredth.

#2

DistroKid — Best Budget Option (If You Don't Mind Annual Fees)

Price: $22.99/year | Royalties: 100% | Stores: 150+

DistroKid is fast and affordable with unlimited uploads. The catch: your music gets removed if you stop paying. YouTube Content ID, Shazam, and other features require paid add-ons ($0.99–$7.95 each). No sync licensing or publishing admin. See our full DistroKid alternative comparison →

Best for: Artists who are okay with annual fees and don't need publishing or sync.

#3

CD Baby — Best for One-Time Payment Fans

Price: $9.95/single, $29/album | Royalties: 91% (9% commission) | Stores: 150+

CD Baby's one-time pricing appeals to artists who hate subscriptions. But 9% of your royalties forever is a steep hidden cost. Publishing admin is an extra $29/year. Delivery times are slow (2–4 weeks). See our CD Baby alternative breakdown →

Best for: Artists who prefer one-time payments and don't mind the royalty cut.

#4

TuneCore — Best Name Recognition

Price: $9.99/single/year, $29.99/album/year | Royalties: 100% | Stores: 150+

TuneCore has been around since 2006, but costs add up fast. A 10-song album costs $29.99/year — every year. If you stop paying, your music stays in stores for 1 year then gets removed. Publishing admin requires a separate TuneCore Publishing subscription. See our TuneCore alternative comparison →

Best for: Artists who value brand recognition over cost savings.

#5

LANDR — Best for Production Tools

Price: $12.99–$35.88/month | Royalties: 100% | Stores: 150+

LANDR bundles distribution with AI mastering, samples, and plugins. But at $23.88/month for full distribution + mastering, it's expensive. Distribution alone starts at $12.99/month — $155/year.

Best for: Producers who want mastering and distribution in one subscription.

#6

Amuse — Best Freemium Distributor

Price: Free (limited) or $49.99/year | Royalties: 100% | Stores: Major platforms

Amuse offers free distribution but with significant limitations: slower delivery times (1–3 weeks), limited analytics, and no playlist pitching on the free tier. Their "Fast Forward" advances are available but smaller than OneSync's.

Best for: Beginners who want to test distribution before committing.

#7

RouteNote — Decent Free Option with a Royalty Trade-Off

Price: Free (15% commission) or $9.99/year (100%) | Royalties: 85% or 100% | Stores: 150+

RouteNote's free tier takes 15% of your royalties — which, as we covered in our free distribution guide, adds up dramatically over time. The paid plan is affordable but basic.

Best for: Artists who are okay giving up 15% to avoid paying anything upfront.
