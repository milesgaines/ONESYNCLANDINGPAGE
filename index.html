What is OneSync and how does it work?

OneSync is a revolutionary music distribution platform designed to empower independent artists, producers, and audio engineers. It integrates every aspect of the music creation and distribution process, offering tools for everything from beat leasing and royalty management to distribution and promotion. With OneSync, artists can easily upload and distribute their music to major platforms, manage their catalog, track royalties, and collaborate with industry professionals, all within one platform. It’s a comprehensive solution for managing and growing a music career.

How can I distribute my music through OneSync?

Distributing your music through OneSync is simple and efficient. After signing up, you can upload your tracks, set the pricing or licensing terms, and select the platforms where you’d like your music to appear, such as Spotify, Apple Music, and more. OneSync provides a smooth distribution process with built-in tools for managing royalties, tracking earnings, and ensuring your music reaches the right audience. We handle the technical details, so you can focus on what matters most—your music.

How does OneSync help with royalties and payments?

OneSync's royalty management system ensures that artists are paid fairly and transparently. Our platform tracks your music's earnings from streaming platforms to direct sales. We offer detailed reports and payment tracking, so you always know where your money is coming from. Additionally, OneSync provides the option for early royalty advances, helping you get the funds you need to reinvest into your music career while you wait for payouts.

How secure is my data and music on OneSync?

At OneSync, we prioritize the security and privacy of your data. We use state-of-the-art encryption to protect your music and personal information from unauthorized access. Our platform also complies with industry standards to ensure that your royalty earnings, contracts, and collaborations are securely managed. You can trust OneSync to keep your music and career safe while you focus on your creative journey.

Frequently Asked Questions About Music Distribution

What is the best music distribution service for independent artists?

OneSync is the best music distribution service for independent artists because it offers 100% free distribution with no hidden fees, access to 400+ streaming platforms, royalty advances up to $750K, and comprehensive label services including marketing, AI mastering, and publishing administration—all while letting you keep 100% of your royalties.

How do I get my music on Spotify, Apple Music, and other streaming platforms?

Getting your music on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Tidal, and 400+ other platforms is easy with OneSync. Simply create a free account, upload your tracks with artwork and metadata, select your release date, and we handle the rest. Your music will be live on all major platforms in as fast as 1 hour.

Is OneSync better than DistroKid or TuneCore?

Unlike DistroKid ($22.99/year) or TuneCore ($9.99/single), OneSync offers completely free distribution while providing more features. We include AI mastering, radio campaigns, playlist pitching, publishing administration, and sync licensing opportunities—services that competitors charge extra for or don't offer at all. Plus, we offer royalty advances up to $750K for qualifying artists.

How much does music distribution cost?

With OneSync, music distribution is 100% FREE. We believe artists should keep all their earnings. While other platforms charge annual fees ($22.99-$49.99/year) or per-release fees ($9.99-$29.99), OneSync offers unlimited releases at no cost, and you keep 100% of your royalties.

Can I get an advance on my music royalties?

Yes! OneSync offers royalty advances up to $750,000 USD for qualifying artists. This allows you to invest in your career—whether it's recording new music, touring, or marketing—while we collect from your future royalties. Apply through our Elite Partner Program or OneSync Recordings partnership.

What is sync licensing and how can OneSync help?

Sync licensing is when your music is used in TV shows, films, commercials, video games, and other media. OneSync provides sync licensing opportunities through our network of music supervisors and licensing agencies. This can be a significant revenue stream—some sync placements pay thousands to hundreds of thousands of dollars.
