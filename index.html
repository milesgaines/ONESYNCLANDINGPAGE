What is OneSync and how does it work?

OneSync is a revolutionary music distribution platform designed to empower independent artists, producers, and audio engineers. It integrates every aspect of the music creation and distribution process, offering tools for everything from beat leasing and royalty management to distribution and promotion. With OneSync, artists can easily upload and distribute their music to major platforms, manage their catalog, track royalties, and collaborate with industry professionals, all within one platform. It’s a comprehensive solution for managing and growing a music career.

How can I distribute my music through OneSync?

Distributing your music through OneSync is simple and efficient. After signing up, you can upload your tracks, set the pricing or licensing terms, and select the platforms where you’d like your music to appear, such as Spotify, Apple Music, and more. OneSync provides a smooth distribution process with built-in tools for managing royalties, tracking earnings, and ensuring your music reaches the right audience. We handle the technical details, so you can focus on what matters most—your music.

How does OneSync help with royalties and payments?

OneSync's royalty management system ensures that artists are paid fairly and transparently. Our platform tracks your music's earnings from streaming platforms to direct sales. We offer detailed reports and payment tracking, so you always know where your money is coming from. Additionally, OneSync provides the option for early royalty advances, helping you get the funds you need to reinvest into your music career while you wait for payouts.

How secure is my data and music on OneSync?

At OneSync, we prioritize the security and privacy of your data. We use state-of-the-art encryption to protect your music and personal information from unauthorized access. Our platform also complies with industry standards to ensure that your royalty earnings, contracts, and collaborations are securely managed. You can trust OneSync to keep your music and career safe while you focus on your creative journey.
