Free Music Distributor for Labels: Manage Your Entire Roster for $0

Independent labels need distribution that scales without scaling costs. Here's how to distribute for your entire roster — free.

For Labels: OneSync offers unlimited artist accounts, unlimited releases, custom label branding, per-artist royalty splits, and full analytics — all free. No per-artist fees. No per-release charges. No royalty commission.

Why Label Distribution Is Expensive (and Doesn't Have to Be)

Most distributors charge per artist, per release, or both. For a label with 10 artists releasing regularly, costs add up fast:

  • DistroKid Label plan: $79.99/year for up to 100 artists. Includes unlimited uploads but adds charges for YouTube Content ID, mastering, etc.
  • TuneCore: Per-release fees for each artist. 10 artists × 3 releases/year × $9.99 = $299.70/year minimum.
  • CD Baby: One-time fees per release + 9% commission on ALL artist royalties. That 9% hits your entire label revenue.

With OneSync: $0. For any number of artists. For any number of releases. Forever.
