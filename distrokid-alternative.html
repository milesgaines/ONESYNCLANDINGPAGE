Best DistroKid Alternative 2026: Why Artists Are Switching

DistroKid is popular — but annual fees, paid add-ons, and missing features are driving artists to look elsewhere. Here's the best alternative.

TL;DR: OneSync offers everything DistroKid does — unlimited uploads, 400+ stores, 100% royalties — plus sync licensing, publishing admin, and $750K advances. And it's completely free.

Why Artists Are Leaving DistroKid

DistroKid revolutionized music distribution with its affordable annual pricing. But in 2026, artists are discovering significant limitations:

  • Music gets removed if you stop paying. Miss a payment? Your entire catalog disappears from Spotify, Apple Music, and every other store. Your streaming history and playlist placements — gone.
  • Add-on fees stack up. YouTube Content ID ($4.95/year per song), Shazam ($0.99/song), custom label name ($5/year), lyrics ($1.99/song), Store Maximizer ($7.95/year). A catalog of 20 songs with full features costs over $150/year.
  • No sync licensing. DistroKid doesn't offer sync placement opportunities. A single TV/film placement can earn $1,000–$500,000+.
  • No publishing administration. If you write your own songs, you're missing royalties without a publisher. DistroKid doesn't offer this service.
  • No royalty advances. Need funding for your next project? DistroKid doesn't offer advances.
  • Customer support issues. Many artists report slow or non-existent support responses.
